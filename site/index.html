ENGINEERING SCOPE

Entirely software-based. ROS REP 103/105 guide units and frames; IPC and ISO references guide documented design intent. The project demonstrates modeled behavior and soft real-time pacing. Physical performance, manufacturing readiness, hard real-time scheduling, and safety certification require further validation. The model has no emergency brake.
